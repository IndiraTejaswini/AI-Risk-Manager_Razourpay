| Group | Positives | Share of positives |
|---|---:|---:|
| Entered fulfillment | 1,182 | 40.00% |
| Never shipped | 1,773 | 60.00% |

**75 of 617 `canceled` positives carry a carrier date** - they shipped and were cancelled in transit. A status-only flag would file them under *never shipped*, which is the wrong side of the line for a delivery-failure label and is exactly the population candidate label (b) turns on.

Cross-check: statuses that structurally cannot have shipped (`approved`, `created`, `invoiced`, `processing`, `unavailable`) carrying a carrier date: **0**. The carrier-date flag and the status vocabulary agree.

## 4. Candidate label definitions

MDD is the minimum detectable PR-AUC difference at that test size, on the same approximation `eval/positive_counts.md` section 6 states, at AP = 0.10 and rho = 0.8.

| Definition | Total positives | Train | Test | Test prevalence | MDD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| (a) any non-delivered | 2,955 | 2,576 | 379 | 1.91% | 0.027 |
| (b) entered fulfillment, never delivered | 1,182 | 1,028 | 154 | 0.77% | 0.043 |
| (c) non-delivered excluding `unavailable` | 2,346 | 1,997 | 349 | 1.75% | 0.028 |

Definition (b) uses the carrier-date flag. Read from status alone (`order_status == 'shipped'`) it would be 1,107 orders, omitting the 75 cancelled-in-transit rows.

Read against the section 2 decision rule, which keys on test positives:

- **(a) any non-delivered** - 379 test positives, well clear of the 150 threshold.
- **(b) entered fulfillment, never delivered** - 154 test positives, above the 150 threshold, but with little headroom.
- **(c) non-delivered excluding `unavailable`** - 349 test positives, well clear of the 150 threshold.

None of the three falls below the gate. The choice is therefore a construct-validity question rather than a sample-size one, and section 3.6 already frames it: (a) is the broadest and the least specific to a delivery failure; (b) is the closest to doorstep refusal and costs roughly 60% of the positive class; (c) sits between them and removes the one status that is unambiguously a stock problem rather than a delivery one. This script reports the counts; it does not pick.
